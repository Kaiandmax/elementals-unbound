How the confluence themes are made

Each dual and triple theme is braided from its parents' themes, the way a braided card is. The first half takes the lead element's opening bars and a partner's answer; the second half takes the rhythm element's tune and the lead's ending. The lead element sets the mode and the lead sound; the others bring the harmony, bass and drums. Every confluence has its own key and tempo.
