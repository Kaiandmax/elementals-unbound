Sound Room

Elementals Unbound's music, as it's written. Each strip plays the main motif in that element's sound, in an enhanced retro style: handheld-RPG writing with a fuller, smoother sound. Switch to Classic chip to hear the strict 8-bit version. Press play; sound starts after your first tap.

The motif: "Unbound"

Nine notes over two bars: an open fifth, a turn around the fourth, then a leap to the octave. It's written as scale degrees, so every element plays the same shape in its own mode. The middle notes are where the modes differ most, which is why Fire and Wind sound so unlike each other.

Direction

Retro in the style of the classic handheld monster-collecting RPGs, but less retro than a real handheld: same vibe, fuller sound (set by the designer, 23 Oct 2026). The tunes are all original. Still open, to explore later:

  • The motif. Keep "Unbound", tweak it, or try another?
  • How retro. Is Enhanced the right distance from the handheld, or should it move further either way?
  • Each element. Does any element sound wrong for its cards? Say which and why.
