# Isa Corran

**Water.** A Covenant archivist from Saltmere, the daughter of a League counting house. Sharp, bookish and out of her depth in the field. She is the first to understand the Unbinding. **Romanceable.**

## Voice

Precise and dry. Talks in footnotes when nervous. Brave in a way she doesn't notice.

- *"Technically that's a Copper rift. Technically, I'm going to be sick."*
- *"Edda Calder. Seal register, page four hundred and twelve. Wren, she sat on the last council."*
- *"Records don't go missing. Records get taken."*

## Arc by act

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| II | **Her chapter (Saltmere).** Finds Edda's name in the seal registers. Joins after Lowmere drowns, to get out of the counting house. |
| III | Finds the border-vault records taken out of the archive under Seraphine's seal. Nobody listens. |
| IV | Her family's house profits from the truce of vaults, and she exposes it. Her proof is what Halden shows Wren. |
| V | Completes Edda's method of sealing: many hands, the Founding Circle's way. The finale depends on her. |

## Loot card (concept for TCG Design)

**The Tolling Kraken** (Bellwater Vault, II-1). The great wild under the Saltmere harbor bell, bound by the city's founders. Isa reads the bell-founders' cipher that opens it.
- **Concept:** a Water Manifest that punishes the enemy for casting. Knowledge as control.
