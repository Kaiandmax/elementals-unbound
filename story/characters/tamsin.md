# Tamsin of Vol

**Lightning.** A runaway acolyte of the Stormcourt's storm-priesthood. Intense and devout, she is the only one who can read the Stormcourt's sealed scripture. **Romanceable.**

## Voice

Formal, earnest, prone to quoting scripture. She takes everything literally, then slowly learns to laugh at that.

- *"The storm keeps no ledger. Isa, why do you keep a ledger?"*
- *"Vol speaks in thunder. Lately, Vol has said nothing at all."*
- *"I prayed to a god. I found a prisoner."*

## Arc by act

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| II | Found hiding in the Holdfast deep roads with stolen scripture, hunted by Stormcourt binders. The party protects her and she joins. On her first night she prays and nobody laughs. Dove braids her hair. |
| III | Watches two armies pray to their own gods before killing each other. Her faith cracks. |
| IV | **Her chapter (Thunderhold).** Her scripture shows that Vol is a Primordial sealed under the cathedral, and that the priesthood and the Hollow mean to unbind it. She chooses duty over faith (written, not a choice) and fights her own god beside Wren. |
| V | Brings the reformed acolytes to the gathering and leads the prayer for the Founding Circle's way of sealing. |

## Loot card (concept for TCG Design)

**The Hymn of Thunderhold** (Thunderhold undercroft, IV-1). A lesser storm wild bound as the cathedral's "voice of Vol". Tamsin was trained to sing to it.
- **Concept:** a Lightning Manifest built around chaining many cards in one turn. A storm of small prayers.
