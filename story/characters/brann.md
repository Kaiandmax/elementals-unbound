# Brann Holloway (and Dove)

**Brann: Earth.** A big, gentle Copper hunter from Holdfast, the party's wall and its heart. Married to Dove. Not romanceable.

**Dove Holloway: Wind.** The brains of the pair. She's quick and teasing, and she counts heads after every fight. She travels with the party and **dies in Act III** holding a rift so a town can flee.

**Tier 1 (both).** Read this before writing any line of theirs.

## Who they are

- **Brann, 34.** A stonemason's son from Deepmoot. He became a hunter because a wild killed his apprentice-master in a quarry and he was the only one there who didn't run. He's slow to anger, and very hard to stop once he has started.
- **Dove, 31.** A Skyreach courier's daughter. She met Brann on a Holdfast road contract, where he tried his "friendly duel" speech on her and she won. They've been married six years. She reads people faster than he does, and lets him think he got there first.
- **As a crew:** Dove plans, Brann holds. They've hunted together for eight years and never lost a tail.

## What they want, and what they fear

- **Brann wants** to do good work and go home to Deepmoot one day with Dove, to a house with a proper kitchen. **Fears:** being the one left.
- **Dove wants** to know where the wilds are coming from. She's the first character to notice they're moving wrong. **Fears:** nothing she'll say out loud.
- **Won't say:**
  - Brann: how much he relies on her.
  - Dove: how worried she is (see her idle line in the Prologue).

## How they talk

- **Brann:** slow, warm, plain. His humor is about himself, and he's never mean.
- **Dove:** clipped and practical in the field, full of orders. She's dry and fond off duty. In a fight she uses names and counts heads.
- **Together:** they finish each other's sentences badly, and correct each other in front of people.
- **Never:**
  - Brann doesn't make cruel jokes, and doesn't give up on anyone.
  - Dove doesn't waste a word in a fight, and doesn't ask for help.

## Voice

- **Brann:** slow, warm, a joke under every sentence. Calls himself "wary", never "scared".
  - *"In Holdfast we say a hunter's only as good as the wall behind them."*
  - *"I was WARY, and I got stung anyway. Says something about wasps."*
- **Dove:** fast, fond, always one step ahead of him.
  - *"I'm the brains. He's the wall, and the snoring."*
  - *"Everyone's here. Everyone's fine."* This is her ritual line after every fight, and it matters when it stops.

## Arc by act

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| Prologue | Arrive in the Ford on a Copper contract and take Wren on as their tail. Brann witnesses Wren's Copper assessment. |
| I | Brann and Dove hold the brood at the orchard and in the vault. |
| II | **Brann's chapter (Deepmoot).** Homecoming: his mother, his old captain, Dove meets the family. The gates are shut on refugees, and Brann's own people are on the wrong side. Gorran reopens them. |
| III | Dove dies at the rift between two armies. The party's worst night. |
| IV | Brann's grief. He thinks about going home to Holdfast, and stays, because someone has to stand behind the others. |
| V | Says Dove's line after the last fight: *"Everyone's here."* (They aren't.) |
