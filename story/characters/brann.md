# Brann Holloway (and Dove)

**Brann: Earth.** A big, gentle Copper hunter from Holdfast, the party's wall and its heart. Married to Dove. Not romanceable.

**Dove Holloway: Wind.** The brains of the pair. She's quick and teasing, and she counts heads after every fight. She travels with the party and **dies in Act III** holding a rift so a town can flee.

## Voice

- **Brann:** slow, warm, a joke under every sentence. Calls himself "wary", never "scared".
  - *"In Holdfast we say a hunter's only as good as the wall behind them."*
  - *"I was WARY, and I got stung anyway. Says something about wasps."*
- **Dove:** fast, fond, always one step ahead of him.
  - *"I'm the brains. He's the wall, and the snoring."*
  - *"Everyone's here. Everyone's fine."* This is her ritual line after every fight, and it matters when it stops.

## Arc by act

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| Prologue | Arrive in the Ford on a Copper contract and take Wren on as their tail. Brann witnesses Wren's Copper assessment. |
| I | Brann and Dove hold the brood at the orchard and in the vault. |
| II | **Brann's chapter (Deepmoot).** Homecoming: his mother, his old captain, Dove meets the family. The gates are shut on refugees, and Brann's own people are on the wrong side. Gorran reopens them. |
| III | Dove dies at the rift between two armies. The party's worst night. |
| IV | Brann's grief. He thinks about going home to Holdfast, and stays, because someone has to stand behind the others. |
| V | Says Dove's line after the last fight: *"Everyone's here."* (They aren't.) |
