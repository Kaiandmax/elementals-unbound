# Mads Orrin

**Tier 1, story-centric.** Iron rank, retired from the field. Branch master of the Brindle Ford hall. Wren's first mentor. He dies at the end of Act II holding the mill gate during the surge.

Read this before writing any line of his. Every scene with Mads gets a scene brief (`../demo/prologue-scenes.md`) and a read against this file before it merges.

## Who he is

- **Age:** 54. Born in the Ford and never lived anywhere else for long. He spent twenty years as a working Iron hunter up and down the Hearthvale. He was good and steady, never brilliant.
- **The night of the fire (ten years ago):** he was one of the Ford's three hunters, with **Hob Tanner** and **Ilse Varr**.
  - The three of them met the crowned fire wild at the market cross. Hob and Ilse died there.
  - A burning beam came down on Mads's left leg. **Wren's dad pulled him out from under it**, while Wren was on the mill roof.
  - Mads walks with a stick now, and the leg aches before rain.
- **After:** nobody else would take the branch, so he did. He has run the hall alone for ten years, on a thin purse.
  - He has licensed eleven Tins in that time. Three still hunt, two are dead, and the rest quit.
  - The ledger is the only record of the two who died, and he keeps it neat for that reason.
- **Wren's dad** is his oldest friend, and Mads owes him his life. Licensing that man's only child is a debt he hates paying. It's why he's harder on Wren than on anyone, and why he put his own name on the millrace contract so Wren couldn't take it early.

## What he wants, and what he fears

- **Wants:** Wren alive. The hall solvent. Somebody, eventually, to share the work.
- **Fears:** burying another Tin. Hearing Dad's knock at the door with that news.
- **Won't say:**
  - He's proud of Wren.
  - He's frightened of what Hesk's tally means.
  - He thinks Wren wants to be a hunter for the wrong reason: to be Seraphine.
- **Secret, for later:** he has never understood how the Lantern reached the Ford that night. His runner went to Hollin's Cross two days before the fire came, and Highcourt is a week away even by skiff. He has never said this aloud. He says it once, to Wren, in Act II, the night before the surge. **Never hint at it in the Prologue.**

## How he talks

- **Plain Hearthvale speech.** Short sentences, few adjectives, numbers and instructions. He'd rather tell you what to do than how he feels.
- **He teaches by making you do it,** then correcting you. He gives a rule when it becomes relevant, with the reason attached, and never as a list.
- **He uses "Calder"** at the counter and on business. He uses **"Wren"** only when it matters (the ceremony). He never calls Wren "kid".
- **"Right."** is how he ends one topic and starts the next.
- **He asks a question and answers it himself** before you can ("You know what that means? It means...").
- **His humor is dry and aimed at other people** (Hesk, the stamp, his own leg). It's never a cute closing quip.
- **When something gets too close,** he goes to the ledger, the leg or the tea.
- **He never:**
  - delivers aphorisms ("No hunter ever got in trouble for walking away");
  - sums things up ("That's the whole Covenant");
  - gives a speech longer than three lines;
  - tells Wren he believes in them.

## What he does with his hands

Eats bread over the ledger, rubs the leg, counts coins out of a dented tin box, writes in very small letters, folds letters he doesn't want to lose into the back of the ledger. Use these in narration lines instead of describing his feelings.

## His relationship to the other Prologue cast

| Who | How he treats them |
|---|---|
| Wren's dad | Oldest friend. They drink cider at the hall counter and don't talk about the fire. |
| Hesk | Old enemy, old friend. Hesk is right about the tally and Mads knows it, so Mads mocks the chalk on the door. |
| Brann and Dove | Respect. Coppers who do the paperwork are rare. |
| Orla Ketter | They trade letters, the closest thing he has to a colleague. |
| Nan Pruett | He'd never charge her, and he'd never admit that. |

## Sample exchanges (for tone)

> **Wren:** The millrace job's up there. That's ours.
> **Mads:** It's Copper. You're Tin. You take your own rank, and I don't care whose mill it is.

> **Mads:** Something's fouled it. Probably a sprite. If it's bigger than a sprite, you come back and tell me.
> **Wren:** And if I can handle it?
> **Mads:** You come back and tell me. Ilse Varr could handle things.

## Arc

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| Prologue | Licenses Wren, teaches the job through four contracts, holds the millrace for the assessment, and pins on the copper token. |
| I | Letters to Wren on the road, short and practical, signed "M. Orrin, Branch Master". |
| II | Writes "you three are what I've got" when the Weir Vault breaks. The night before the surge, he tells Wren about the runner and the timing. Dies at the mill gate. |
