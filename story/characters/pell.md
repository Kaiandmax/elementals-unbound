# Pell Varrane

**Fire.** Second son of a Pyrrhen war-house. Gifted, arrogant, and impatient to rise. He's Wren's rival in Act I and a companion from the end of Act I. **Romanceable.**

## Voice

Fast, flashy, defensive. He insults you instead of saying thank you, and gives useful advice disguised as an insult. Never admits he's sulking.

- *"Remember the name, it'll be on the Gold board within the year."*
- *"Your Reactions are sloppy. You held Energy back and then never used it. Just saying."*
- *"I'm not shouting, I'm COMMANDING."*

## Family

His elder brother is **Captain Davor Varrane**, an officer in Vexis's relief columns, polite and quietly ruthless. His father writes to Seraphine, and his mother writes to Pell, though he never answers.

## Arc by act

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| Prologue | Steps off the evening barge an hour after Wren makes Copper, and is unimpressed. |
| I | Rival duel at Hollin's Cross. Invites himself to the Weir Vault "for the pay". |
| II | Wants the Highcourt tournament badly, and loses to Aeris with good grace, to everyone's surprise. Tells Kes his secret doesn't matter. |
| III | **His chapter.** Davor's column is ordered to burn a spring town, and Pell stands against his brother in a duel. Davor disowns him. Pell chooses the Covenant. |
| IV | Faces Davor again at the occupied Ford. He wins, and doesn't finish it. |
| V | Holds a Primordial's brood in the last vault. *"Don't make it weird."* |

## Loot card (concept for TCG Design)

**The Varrane Crest-Drake.** The war-house's ancestral Fire binding, sealed in the Steam Springs vault (III-2), which the Varranes have guarded for generations. Pell takes it when he breaks with his family.
- **Concept:** a Fire Manifest that rewards attacking alone, one creature against the odds. Pell's pride, made useful.
