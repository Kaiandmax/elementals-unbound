# Kes Arden

**Wind.** A sky-courier who knows every sky-road, quick-tongued and charming. Secretly **Prince Kestrel Arden** of Skyreach, disinherited. **Romanceable.**

## Voice

Light, teasing, deflecting. He lies easily about small things and never about people. When he finally gets serious, the whole party goes quiet.

- *"Courier rates. Plus danger money. Plus I like you, so minus danger money."*
- *"I'm from nowhere special. Nowhere special has very nice gardens."*
- *"My uncle sold the sky. I'm going to buy it back."*

## Arc by act

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| II | Flies the party out of drowned Lowmere and stays "to see the job through". In Highcourt his past catches up: his uncle, the ruling prince, offers to buy the whole party. He tells the party the truth at camp. |
| IV | **His chapter (Highcourt).** Reclaims his name to stop Skyreach signing its vault over to Seraphine. He wins the vote and loses the vault anyway, to Neris and Seraphine. |
| V | Brings Skyreach's restored court to the gathering. |

## Loot card (concept for TCG Design)

**The Windlord's Roc** (Galewright Vault, II-3). Sealed by Aeris Calloway's teacher in the oldest of the Highcourt islands. Kes knew the way in as a boy.
- **Concept:** a Wind Manifest that rewards returning your own creatures to hand and replaying them. Kes always comes back.

- Teaches Tamsin card tricks.
- Flirts with everyone, sincerely with only one person, if the player chooses him.
