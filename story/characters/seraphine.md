# Seraphine Vael, "the Lantern"

**Aurora.** First of the Seven Exalted and the most beloved person on Aster. She saved Brindle Ford when Wren was eight. She is the **final antagonist**, and she believes she is saving the world.

## Voice

Warm, attentive, unhurried. She remembers everyone's name. She never raises her voice. Her light is cold green and gold, like the sky in winter.

- *"You held your ground. That's all any of us can do."* (cold open)
- *"I remember everyone I couldn't save, and everyone I could. It helps to remember the second kind."*
- *"Tell me. Was anything taken from the Vault?"* (the first clue)
- *"I'm not asking you to agree. I'm asking you to count."* (Act V)

## What she wants, and why

Seraphine has known for years that the Founding Circle's seals cannot hold. Her answer is to gather every Primordial binding before the Hollow or any crown can, rebind them all into herself, and become the one seal: a single keeper with the power of the Sundering, deciding which lands are worth protecting.

To get the vaults, she lets border towns fall so that terrified nations will hand them over. She keeps lists of those towns. She grieves every one.

## Clues to plant (a second playthrough should catch them)

| Act | Clue |
|---|---|
| Cold open | She arrives exactly when the Ford is half burned, not before. |
| I | At the Weir Vault: "Was anything taken?" A pause on Edda Calder's name. "Keep it close." |
| II | Lowmere drowns; she "couldn't reach it in time". She recommends Wren for Steel, which keeps Wren busy and grateful. |
| III | Her vote keeps the Covenant out of the war. Isa finds the border-vault registers taken out under her seal. |
| IV | The truce of vaults is her proposal. Halden's silences. |
| V | The Founding Vault is under the Ford. She was there for the vault, ten years ago. |

## Arc by act

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| Cold open | Walks out of the smoke and saves the Ford. |
| I | At the Weir Vault gate she's wonderful to everyone, and asks one question a beat too carefully. |
| II–III | Glimpsed from afar, always helping, always too late. |
| IV | The nations hand her their vaults. Halden tells Wren the truth. |
| V | The reveal is a conversation between equals: she shows Wren her lists and asks Wren to join her. Wren refuses. The final duel, then her end, which is written so it doesn't feel like a villain's. |

## Loot card

None. Her power is what the finale stops.

## Music

Her leitmotif, *Seraphine (The Lantern)*, is the most comforting music in the game. It turns to minor for the final duel.
