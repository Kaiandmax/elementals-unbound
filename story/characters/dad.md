# Owen Calder (Dad)

**Tier 1, story-centric.** Wren's father, the miller at Calder Mill in Brindle Ford. Called "Dad" on screen; "Owen" by Mads, Hesk and the town.

Read this before writing any line of his.

## Who he is

- **Age:** 47. A miller, like his father. Edda Calder was his grandmother.
- **Wren's mother, Bryn,** died of a winter fever when Wren was five. He doesn't talk about her except through the mill: she rebuilt the sluice gate the year before she died, and he still oils it the way she showed him.
- **The night of the fire:**
  - He put Wren on the slate mill roof with a bucket because it was the only roof that wouldn't burn.
  - Then he ran into the square and lifted the beam off Mads.
  - He has never told Wren he was frightened. He tells the story, when he tells it at all, as if it were about Mads.
- **Mads:** they've been friends since they were boys. They drink cider at the hall counter and don't talk about the fire.
- **Wren becoming a hunter:** he has known for ten years it was coming. He went to the hall the night before licence day and asked Mads not to do it, and Mads said no. He isn't angry with Mads. He's angry with the fire.

## What he wants, and what he fears

- **Wants:** Wren safe. The wheel turning. One evening a week where nothing is wrong.
- **Fears:** the hall door opening with Mads behind it, holding his hat.
- **Won't say:**
  - He's proud of Wren.
  - He's scared.
  - He misses Bryn every day.
  - All three come out sideways, through the wheel, food, or Gran Edda.

## How he talks

- **Practical and warm.** Short sentences about real things: flour, the sluice, the weather, supper.
- **Concrete senses:** what he hears at night (the thing gulping in the race), what the wheel sounds like when it's right.
- **He states his position once and then helps anyway.** *"I don't like it. I'm not going to stop you."*
- **Mild teasing,** never sarcasm. He calls Wren "love" when worried. That's gender-neutral in Hearthvale speech, so it's safe for either pick.
- **Never:** lectures, tells Wren what to feel, or makes a speech about duty or danger. He doesn't cry on screen in the Prologue.

## Sample exchanges (for tone)

> **Dad:** I asked him not to license you, you know. Sat in the hall and asked him.
> **Dad:** First time in thirty years he's told me no.
> **Dad:** It comes up under the sluice gate, on the left. It always comes up on the left.

> **Dad:** The wheel's turning. I keep stopping to listen to it.

## Arc

| Act | Beat |
|---|---|
| Prologue | Asks Mads not to license Wren, then helps Wren beat Gullet anyway. Plants Gran Edda's weir story. |
| II | The surge. He keeps the mill open as a shelter. After Mads dies, he's the one who closes Mads's ledger. |
| IV | The occupied Ford: he refuses to grind for the Pyrrhen garrison, and Davor's men take the mill. Wren gets it back. |
| V | He's in the crowd when the Ford answers the call. He brings the flour. |
