# Outline — Elementals Unbound

Owner: Story. **Status: first draft.** This is the plot in the order the player meets it, by act and chapter. It follows [`bible.md`](bible.md): one authored story with no branching, where each act covers a rank band and ends on a boss above Wren's rank, followed by a promotion.

For each chapter:

- **Place:** where it happens.
- **Rank:** Wren's rank when it starts.
- **Above:** world events that happen over Wren's head.
- **Beats:** what the player does.
- **Vault:** the dungeon, its sealed great wild, and the loot card concept.
- **Boss:** the boss, with its crown taken from the menu in `design/bosses.md`.
- **Camp:** the party conversation after the big beat.

Loot cards are **story concepts**: TCG Design designs the effect and Balance sets the numbers. Each one should teach a way of playing the starter decks don't.

## Prologue and Act I: Notice Boards (Tin → Iron)

Already written as the playable demo: [`demo/act1.md`](demo/act1.md), with all its text in [`data/act1.js`](data/act1.js).

In brief: the cold open; the licence; Gullet (**Copper**); Brann, Dove and Pell; Hollin's Cross and Aeris; the Cinder Mother on a rift (**Iron**); the Hollow at the Weir Vault; the Sluicewarden; **Slackwater**; and Seraphine at the gate.

## Act II: The Wider World (Iron → Steel)

The party travels the three nations nearest the Hearthvale, one after another, and grows from three to six. Each nation is a self-contained story. **Tone:** wonder, with the first real costs.

**Opening news (Above):** a broadsheet at Hollin's Cross reports that the Hollow broke a vault in the Skyreach foothills. Seraphine's name is in the second paragraph, arriving too late.

### II-1. Saltmere (Tidewater League, Water)

- **Place:** Saltmere, a city of canals, bells and merchant houses. The League "sells protection" to river towns: Covenant hunters hired at League prices, so poorer towns go without.
- **Beats:**
  - River-barge contracts.
  - Wren meets **Isa Corran** in the Covenant archive. She is chasing records of the Founding Circle and finds **Edda Calder's** name on a seal register.
  - A river town, **Lowmere**, can't pay. The party takes its contract for free and arrives to find it half drowned; Seraphine "couldn't reach it in time" (thread: Seraphine).
  - Isa joins, partly to get out of her family's counting house.
- **Vault:** the Bellwater Vault under the Saltmere harbor. Inside is **the Tolling Kraken**, a great wild that the city's founders bound and built the harbor bell over.
  - **Loot concept:** a Water Manifest that punishes the enemy for casting (it "tolls" whenever they cast something), a control build-around.
- **Boss:** a crowned harbor wild, **Tide** crown (it returns your biggest creature to hand). **Steel** rank against Wren's Iron.
- **Kes:** **Kes Arden** is the sky-courier who flies the party out of the drowned town. He's charming, and he lies about where he's from. He joins to "see the job through".
- **Camp:** Isa and Pell argue about whether the League is evil or just greedy. Brann settles it with stew.

### II-2. Deepmoot (Kingdom of Holdfast, Earth)

- **Place:** Deepmoot, halls carved inside a mountain, home to Brann. Holdfast has shut its gates to Hearthvale refugees, so Brann's own family is on the wrong side of a wall.
- **Beats:**
  - Brann's homecoming: his mother, his old Iron captain, Dove meeting the family. It's warm and funny.
  - Deep-road contracts.
  - The party finds **Tamsin**, a runaway storm acolyte from Vol, hiding in the deep roads with stolen scripture. Stormcourt hunters are after her. The party protects her, and she joins.
- **Vault:** the Root Vault under Deepmoot. The kingdom keeps it secret and treats it as a national treasure. Inside is **the Rootbound Colossus**, sealed by Earthbinder Gorr, an ancestor of Gorran.
  - The Hollow breach it during the visit. The party fights down while Holdfast's king blames the Covenant.
  - **Loot concept:** an Earth Manifest that grows each turn you don't attack, a patient-defense build-around.
- **Boss:** **Sealwork** again, but a crowned version: the Colossus's own warden-root. **Steel** rank.
- **Above:** the Exalted **Gorran Stoneheart** arrives after the battle and quietly makes the king reopen the gates. It's the first time Wren sees an Exalted use power for the small folk, and it sets up Gorran as an ally.
- **Camp:** Tamsin's first night. She prays to Vol and nobody laughs. Dove braids her hair.

### II-3. Highcourt (Skyreach Principalities, Wind)

- **Place:** Highcourt, floating courts on sky-anchored islands. Its princes bid for Covenant champions like racehorses.
- **Beats:**
  - Kes's past catches up: he is **Prince Kestrel Arden**, disinherited. His uncle, now the ruling prince, offers to buy the whole party.
  - A tournament of Covenant duels (NPC duels at the top AI level). Pell wants to win it, and **Aeris Calloway** is the reigning champion.
  - **Wren promoted to Steel** after the tournament semi-final.
- **Vault:** the Galewright Vault inside the oldest island. Inside is **the Windlord's Roc**, sealed by Aeris's teacher.
  - **Loot concept:** a Wind Manifest that rewards returning your own creatures to hand and replaying them, Wind's real identity.
- **Boss:** a **duel against Aeris Calloway** (exhibition, top AI level, no phases). It's written to be lost: Aeris is an Exalted, Wren is Iron. If the player does win, Aeris gets a different line; nothing else changes.
  - Then the real fight: the Hollow ground the island, and the party has to stop the fall. Boss: a crowned wind wild, **Unbound** crown (its biggest creature can't be blocked). **Silver** rank, against Wren's Steel.
- **Camp:** Kes tells the truth to the party. Pell, of all people, is the one who tells him it doesn't matter.

### II-4. The Ford Holds (finale)

- **Place:** Brindle Ford.
- **Beats:**
  - Word comes that a rift-surge is moving down the river. Every hunter is gone east; the Ford has Mads and a hall full of Tins.
  - The party races home.
  - The Ford is defended in a sequence of duels across town, and the companions hold the side fights.
  - **Mads dies** at the mill gate holding the last line. Wren's last duel is the surge's heart, **Brood** crown, **Silver** rank.
- **After:**
  - The wheel turns.
  - Tilly asks to be apprenticed at the hall.
  - Wren chooses to keep the party together and swears the Covenant oath again, in Mads's words.
- **Music:** Mads's death needs its own cue (flagged to Music).

## Act III: Fire on the Border (Silver → Gold)

War comes. The party fights as Covenant neutrals, protecting people on both sides. **Tone:** the cost of duty, gray choices, the first doubts about the people at the top.

**Opening news (Above):** Vexis declares war on the League over the river tolls. The Seven vote 4–3 to keep the Covenant neutral, with Seraphine casting the deciding vote.

### III-1. The Forge (Hollin's Cross, then the borderlands)

- **Beats:**
  - **Forge licence** at Silver, and the first braid (fusion tutorial).
  - **Corvane Ashe** retires from the Seven in disgust at the vote and takes Wren as a student.
  - From here on he is Wren's mentor, the one who questions every ideal.

### III-2. Steam Springs (Steam, League–Dominion border)

- **Beats:**
  - The hot-spring towns sit between two armies.
  - The party escorts refugees.
  - **Pell's choice:** his brother Davor's column is ordered to burn a spring town. Pell stands against his brother in a duel at the top AI level, and Davor never forgives him.
- **Vault:** a Steam vault under the springs, guarded for generations by the Varranes. **Loot:** the **Varrane Crest-Drake**, Pell's personal card (see `characters/pell.md`): a Fire Manifest that rewards attacking alone.
- **Boss:** a crowned steam wild, **Overcharge** crown. **Gold** rank.

### III-3. The Ashfields (Volcano / Wildfire, the Dominion)

- **Beats:**
  - Behind Pyrrhen lines, to free Covenant hunters held as "advisors".
  - **Cindergate** itself appears, and so does **Vexis**: a direct, open villain, impressive and terrible.
  - The party escapes rather than wins.
- **Vault:** an Ash vault. **Loot concept:** an Ash Manifest whose tokens burn out and rise again.
- **Boss:** **Vexis, the Pyrelord**, at the top AI level. This is an escape duel: survive a set number of turns. That's a TCG Design question, flagged.

### III-4. The Rift at Two Armies (finale)

- **Beats:**
  - Both armies' war magic tears a huge rift on the border.
  - The Covenant hunters of both sides have to work together, and the party leads them.
  - **Dove dies** holding the rift's second front so a town can flee. It's written, not a choice, and it's Brann's grief arc.
- **Boss:** a crowned wild that both armies woke, **Twin Call** crown. **Adamant** rank, against Wren's Gold.
- **After:** **promoted to Gold**. Isa finds that the Founding seal registers for the border vaults were taken from the archive with Seraphine's seal on the request, though nobody notices yet.
- **Camp:** the hardest scene in the game. Brann. Everyone.

## Act IV: Crowns (Adamant)

The world is breaking. Wren fights beside the Exalted as a peer. **Tone:** epic, and the cracks at the top show.

**Opening news (Above):** rift-surges in every nation. A truce is signed in Highcourt, and the price is that every nation hands its Primordial vault to Seraphine "for safekeeping".

### IV-1. Thunderhold (Stormcourt of Vol, Lightning)

- **Beats:**
  - Tamsin's chapter. Her scripture shows that **Vol** is a Primordial sealed under the cathedral, and that the priesthood and the Hollow mean to unbind their god.
  - Tamsin has to choose between faith and duty; she chooses duty, and it is written.
- **Vault:** the Thunderhold undercroft. **Loot concept:** a Lightning Manifest built around chaining many cards in one turn.
- **Boss:** half of Vol unbound, the first Primordial fight. **Exalted** rank, fought alongside **Ysil of the White Reach**. Vol is stopped, not sealed. **Promoted to Adamant**.

### IV-2. The Truce of Vaults (Highcourt)

- **Beats:**
  - Kes's chapter. He reclaims his name to stop Skyreach from signing its vault over.
  - The party wins the vote and loses the vault anyway: Neris Saltwind and Seraphine take it "for safety".
  - **Triple braids** unlock.
- **Boss:** a duel against **Neris Saltwind**, an Exalted, at the top AI level.

### IV-3. What Halden Knows

- **Beats:**
  - **Halden Mirr**, the Covenant's spymaster, meets Wren in secret and tells the truth: Seraphine's plan to become the one seal. There's the proof Isa found, and the towns she let fall, Lowmere included.
  - Wren's hero-worship breaks.
- **Camp:** Wren alone with Corvane. *"Rank is a leash the nations let us hold."* Wren finds an answer of their own: the Founding Circle sealed together.

### IV-4. Brindle Ford, Occupied

- **Beats:**
  - Pyrrhen troops hold the Ford "for its protection". The party frees it.
  - Tilly is Tin, then Copper; she fights her first real duel beside Wren.
  - Pell faces Davor one last time.
- **Boss:** Captain Davor Varrane, at the top AI level.

## Act V: The Seventh Seat (Exalted)

**Tone:** the gathering, the confrontation, the cost. Then home.

### V-1. The Seventh Seat (Highcourt, the Covenant council hall)

- **Beats:** Wren is raised to **Exalted**, taking Corvane's empty seat. Wren goes to Seraphine as a peer, and the conversation is the reveal.
- **Seraphine:** she isn't a monster. She shows Wren the failing seals and the lists of towns. She asks Wren to join her, and Wren refuses.
- **Above:** the council breaks. Gorran and Ysil stand with Wren, Neris with Seraphine, and Aeris wavers until the last chapter.

### V-2. Every Hunter Answers

- **Beats:** the gathering, written as the payoff of the main plot. Everyone Wren helped answers the call:
  - Jory and the Hollin's Cross hall.
  - Lowmere's survivors.
  - The Holdfast gate-guards and Gorran.
  - Kes's restored court.
  - Tamsin's reformed acolytes.
  - Tilly.
  - Bound great wilds: Slackwater, Wren's own first loot card, is part of the story.
- **Isa** completes Edda's method of sealing.

### V-3. The Last Vault

- **Place:** the Founding Vault under the Hearthvale. It was under the Ford all along, which is why Seraphine asked about the Weir Vault.
- **Beats:** the companions hold the side fights (Brann, Pell, Isa, Kes and Tamsin, each against a Primordial's brood) while Wren goes for the heart.
- **Bosses:**
  - A Primordial gauntlet.
  - **Seraphine, the Lantern**: her own theme turned to minor, every crown at once (a TCG Design question).
- **Ending:**
  - The Primordials are resealed by many hands, the Founding Circle's way.
  - Wren takes the seat for real. The Ford's wheel turns.
  - Tilly asks Wren for her first contract.
  - The romance partner, if chosen, has their beat.

## What other roles will need from this outline

- **TCG Design:**
  - Six loot-card concepts. Five are also the companions' personal cards: the Tolling Kraken (Isa), the Rootbound Colossus (Brann), the Windlord's Roc (Kes), the Varrane Crest-Drake (Pell) and the Hymn of Thunderhold (Tamsin). The sixth is the Ash vault card. Slackwater is already done. See `characters/`.
  - Bosses and crowns per chapter.
  - Two special duels: the **Vexis escape** (survive N turns) and **Seraphine** (every crown at once).
- **Game Dev:** about 15 regions of maps over the game; returning to Brindle Ford with a changed map in each act.
- **Music:**
  - Mads's death (II-4) and Dove's death (III-4).
  - Highcourt's tournament.
  - Thunderhold's cathedral.
  - The council hall.
  - The Founding Vault.
- **Balance:** each act's rank band and the bosses one or two ranks above, as in the bible.
