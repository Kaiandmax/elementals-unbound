# Prologue scene briefs

The scene briefs for the Prologue's tier-1 and tier-2 scenes (see the bible's *Writing tiers*). For each scene, the brief records what each character wants, what they aren't saying, and what has changed by the end. The lines themselves are in `../data/prologue.js`. Character dossiers are in `../characters/`.

## Cold open (`pro_intro`)

- **Point:** Wren's reason for becoming a hunter, and the first sight of Seraphine.
- **Planted:**
  - Dad pulling Mads out from under the beam, so the player knows the two men are bound together before meeting Mads.
  - Two of the three hunters dying.
- **Changes:** the eight-year-old decides what to be.

## Licence Day (`pro_mads_licence`)

- **Mads wants** to get the swearing done and set Wren on something small and safe. **Not saying:** Dad came to the hall last night and asked him not to license Wren, and Mads refused him. That's the first time in thirty years he's refused Dad anything.
- **Wren wants** the millrace contract, because it's family.
- **Changes:** Wren is a Tin. The first rule (take your own rank) arrives because Wren asks for the millrace.

## Report 1 (`pro_mads_report_1`)

- **Mads wants** Wren to learn the ledger is the job. **Not saying:** mouse eggs this late in the year frighten him, because they fit Hesk's tally.
- **Changes:** Wren writes a first ledger line and is made to add what was actually seen. The third rule (walk away if it's bigger than the notice) arrives with Ilse Varr's name, and nothing more.

## Report 2 (`pro_mads_report_2`)

- **Mads wants** to pay Wren without Wren seeing how little is in the box. He fails.
- **Wren wants** to know whether bringing eggs was wrong.
- **Changes:** Wren learns that poor jobs are paid by rich ones, and that this month there aren't any rich ones. Mads takes two eggs as his "share", which is a kindness he won't name.

## The crew (`pro_brann_dove_arrive`, `pro_dove_brief`, `pro_pack_leader`, `pro_straggler_win`, `pro_report_3`)

- **Brann wants** the job done and nobody hurt, and he's pleased to have a tail. **Dove wants** a tail who listens. **Not saying (Dove):** the hounds are too far south, and that worries her more than the pack does.
- **Mads wants** Wren to see a crew work properly.
- **Changes:** Wren sees calm competence for the first time since the cold open. In the report, Mads makes Wren write Dove's praise in the ledger, which echoes Report 1.

## The letters (`pro_mads_letters`, `pro_orla`, `pro_davor`, `pro_report_4`)

- **Mads wants** Orla's count of the Pyrrhen soldiers, and doesn't want Wren to know why yet.
- **Orla wants** the hall to take the soldiers seriously.
- **Davor wants** Covenant goodwill, and to be seen being friendly.
- **Changes:** the world above Wren's head arrives. Mads reads the letter standing up, which he never does.

## The assessment (`pro_mads_assessment`, `pro_dad_assessment`, `pro_brann_witness`)

- **Mads wants** to give Wren the hardest honest advice he can about Gullet without doing the job for them.
- **Dad wants** to stop it and won't. He finally admits that he asked Mads not to license Wren, and Mads said no.
- **Brann wants** to be fair.
- **Why it's Wren's job:** Brann and Dove sat at the sluice two nights and Gullet never surfaced, because it knows a Copper when it sees one. It doesn't know Wren. That's why the hall holds it for a Tin's assessment rather than handing it to the crew, and why Mads holding it isn't neglect of Dad.
- **Changes:** Wren works out what the deck needs ("it hides"), and Mads only confirms it.

## The ceremony (`pro_ceremony`)

- **Mads wants** to do it properly, by the book, in front of the town. Then he wants Wren to go to Dad.
- **Changes:** Mads uses "Wren" instead of "Calder" for the first time. That's the whole emotional beat; he doesn't make a speech.

## Pell (`pro_pell_arrives`)

- **Pell wants** Coppers for Jory Fenn, and to be impressive. **Not saying:** he volunteered for this errand to be far from his brother, and his brother is camped twelve miles away.
- **Changes:** the rival arrives, and Act I's pull downriver begins.

## Dad at night (`pro_dad_evening`)

- **Dad wants** to say he's proud, and can't, so he talks about the wheel and Gran Edda.
- **Changes:** the Weir Vault is planted.
