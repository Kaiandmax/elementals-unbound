# Highcourt and the sky-islands

**Element:** Wind. **Ranks:** Iron; the boss is Steel. (Steel itself comes later, after the Ford surge.) **Chapter:** II-3. **Companions:** Kes's past catches up with him.

## Look and sound

- Islands floating on sky-anchors, linked by sky-roads and courier skiffs.
- White sails, gardens on cliff edges, court dances, wind-chimes everywhere.
- Vain, beautiful, a little cruel.
- Music: *Highcourt*; the tournament uses *Battle: Tournament*.

## Places

| Place | What's there | People |
|---|---|---|
| **Highcourt** | The Prince's Court, the Covenant tourney ground, the sky-docks | Prince **Aldric Arden** (Kes's uncle, ruling prince), courtiers, champions |
| **The Courier Roads** | Sky-roads between islands, wild-haunted | Courier guild captains who know Kes as "Kes" |
| **The Old Island** | The oldest, highest island, holding the Galewright Vault | Aeris Calloway's teacher's memorial |

## Politics

The **Skyreach Principalities** buy prestige.

- **What they want from the Unbinding:** the princes bid for Covenant champions like racehorses. A prince with an Exalted on retainer outranks one without.
- **Kes's family:** Kes's mother was the true heir. She was exiled, then died, and Aldric took the throne and disinherited her son.
- **What Wren learns:** how the Covenant's neutrality is bought and sold. Exalted take patronage, and Aeris owes favors.

## Story beats

1. **Arrival.** Kes flies them in and refuses to land at the Court docks. Nobody asks why, yet.
2. **The tournament.** Covenant duels for patronage, all NPC duels at the top AI level. Pell wants to win it badly.
3. **Kes unmasked.** Aldric recognizes him: *Prince Kestrel*. He offers to buy the whole party, or to expose Kes as a pretender.
4. **Semi-final:** Wren makes the last four, the furthest an Iron has gone in years.
5. **Exhibition against Aeris Calloway.** It's written to be lost: Aeris is an Exalted, and Wren is Iron. If the player wins anyway, Aeris gets a different line. He laughs, and so does the crowd.
6. **The fall.** The Hollow cut the Old Island's anchor, and the island starts to fall toward the city. The party dives into the Galewright Vault to reseat the anchor.
7. **Camp.** Kes tells the truth. Pell, of all people, tells him it doesn't matter.

## The vault

The **Galewright Vault**, inside the Old Island.

- **What's sealed there:** **the Windlord's Roc**, a great sky wild that carried the first island up. It was sealed by Aeris Calloway's teacher, the last Windlord before him.
- **What guards it:** wind-locks that open only in flight, a maze of updrafts, and the Roc's own storm.
- **How the party gets in:** Kes knew the way in as a boy, when he played in the forbidden places.
- **The loot card (Kes's personal card):** the Windlord's Roc, a Wind Manifest concept that rewards returning your creatures to hand and replaying them.

## Boss

**A crowned wind wild**, risen with the Hollow's breach.

- **Rank:** Steel, against Wren's Iron.
- **Crown:** **Unbound**. Its biggest creature can't be blocked. The story reason: you can't wall a storm.
- **Side fight:** Kes and Brann hold the anchor-chains while Wren fights.

- **Act IV:** Kes's chapter. He reclaims his name to stop Skyreach signing its vault over to Seraphine. He wins the vote and loses the vault anyway.
- **Act V:** The Covenant council hall is here, where Wren takes the seventh seat.
