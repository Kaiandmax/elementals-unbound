# Deepmoot and the deep roads

**Element:** Earth. **Ranks:** Iron to Steel; the act boss is Steel. **Chapter:** II-2. **Companions:** Brann's home chapter; Tamsin joins.

## Look and sound

- Halls carved inside a mountain, lit by crystal and forge-glow.
- Stair-towns and the deep roads that run between them.
- A great outer gate, now shut.
- Slow, proud, warm inside, cold at the wall.
- Music: *Deepmoot*.

## Places

| Place | What's there | People |
|---|---|---|
| **The Outer Gate** | The shut gate and a refugee camp of Hearthvale folk outside it | Gate-captain **Hadda Rook**, Brann's old Iron captain |
| **Deepmoot** | The Moot-hall, stair-markets, the Holloway family's stone house | Brann's mother **Ama Holloway**, his little brothers, King **Orvel of Holdfast** |
| **The Deep Roads** | Tunnels between holds, wild-haunted | Tamsin, in hiding; Stormcourt hunters chasing her |
| **The Root Vault** | Under the Moot-hall, a national secret | The Colossus's warden-root |

## Politics

The **Kingdom of Holdfast** trusts walls.

- **What it wants from the Unbinding:** safety. It has shut its gates to Hearthvale refugees.
- **Its vault:** it keeps the Primordial-era vault under Deepmoot as a national treasure, and doesn't tell the Covenant.
- **What Wren learns:** a good people doing a cruel thing out of fear. Brann's own family is inside, and his neighbors are outside.

## Story beats

1. **The shut gate.** The party gets in on Brann's name; the refugees don't. Hadda lets them through, ashamed.
2. **Homecoming.** Ama Holloway feeds everyone. Dove meets the little brothers. It's the warmest, funniest stretch of Act II.
3. **The deep roads.** Contracts lead the party to **Tamsin**, a runaway Stormcourt acolyte with stolen scripture. The party fights off her hunters, and she joins.
4. **The breach.** The Hollow break the Root Vault during the party's visit. King Orvel blames the Covenant, and the party fights down to stop it.
5. **Gorran.** After the battle the Exalted **Gorran Stoneheart** arrives and quietly makes the king reopen the gates. It's the first time Wren sees an Exalted use their power for small folk.

## The vault

The **Root Vault**, under the Moot-hall.

- **What's sealed there:** **the Rootbound Colossus**, sealed by **Earthbinder Gorr** (Gorran's ancestor) in the age after the Sundering. It's a mountain-sized wild that slept as long as nothing disturbed its roots.
- **Who keeps it:** the Holloway family have been its hereditary keepers for six generations. Brann never knew what they were keeping.
- **What guards it:** the warden-root, a living root-wall that rebuilds; stone ward-doors; deep wilds.
- **The loot card (Brann's personal card):** the Rootbound Colossus, an Earth Manifest concept that grows every turn you don't attack.

## Boss

**The warden-root of the Colossus.**

- **Rank:** Steel.
- **Crown:** **Sealwork**, a deliberate echo of the Sluicewarden. Gorr and Edda, it turns out, studied under the same teacher. Its strongest creature rebuilds at each break.
- **Side fight:** Dove and Tamsin hold the Hollow cultists at the door.
