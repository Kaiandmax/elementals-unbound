# The Hearthvale

**Element:** mixed; every element's wilds turn up here. **Ranks:** Tin to Iron in Act I; the player returns in every act. **Home.**

## Look and sound

- River farmland, watermills, apple orchards and market towns.
- Willows, cart ruts, lanterns strung over the river road at festivals.
- It's nobody's capital and everybody's road.
- Music: *Brindle Ford* (home), *Hearthvale Roads*, *Hollin's Cross*, *Homecoming at Dusk*.

## Politics

The Hearthvale has no army. The nations around it each claim to protect it:

- Pyrrhen "relief columns" stand guard at its wells (Act I).
- The League offers river protection at a price (Act II).
- The Dominion occupies the Ford "for its safety" (Act IV).

The Covenant hall is the only power that belongs to the Hearthvale.

## The vault

The **Weir Vault**, built about sixty years ago by Edda Calder, Wren's great-grandmother, to hold **Slackwater, the Weir Serpent**. She bound it in a flood year instead of killing it. It is guarded by the Sluicewarden, a stone-and-water golem she built. The Hollow broke its seal in Act I.
