# Saltmere and the League rivers

**Element:** Water. **Ranks:** Copper; the boss is Iron, and the **Iron assessment** happens here. **Chapter:** II-1. **Companions:** Isa joins; Kes appears.

## Look and sound

- A city of canals, bells and merchant houses on the mouth of the great river.
- Narrow boats, counting-house windows, and the harbor bell that rings every hour.
- Rich and lovely on top, damp and crowded below.
- Music: *Saltmere*; the vault uses *The Vaults*.

## Places

| Place | What's there | People |
|---|---|---|
| **Saltmere** | Canals, the Covenant archive (the largest in the world), the counting houses, the harbor | Isa Corran; her mother **Maren Corran**, head of House Corran; the archive's warden |
| **The Bellwater Harbor** | The harbor bell, and the vault beneath it | Bell-ringers who know old songs |
| **Lowmere** | A poor river town upstream that can't afford League protection | Lowmere's reeve; survivors who return in Act V |

## Politics

The **Tidewater League** is a council of merchant houses that controls the rivers and the ports.

- **What it wants from the Unbinding:** leverage. It hires Covenant hunters at League prices and sells "protection" to river towns.
- **Who pays:** towns that can't pay go without, and their contracts sit on the board unpaid.
- **What Wren learns:** greed without villainy. Nobody in Saltmere wants Lowmere to drown. They just didn't price it in.

## Story beats

1. **River-barge contracts** into Saltmere. The barge-woman from the Hearthvale side contract is the ferry captain.
2. **The archive.** Wren meets **Isa Corran** among the restricted shelves. She finds **Edda Calder** on a seal register: Edda sat on the Founding Circle's last council.
3. **Lowmere's unpaid contract.** The party takes it for free and arrives to find the town half drowned by a crowned wild. Seraphine "couldn't reach it in time" (first Act II clue).
4. **Kes Arden**, a sky-courier, flies survivors out and stays "to see the job through".
5. **Isa joins**, partly to get out of the counting house. Her mother lets her go with a ledger entry.
6. **The Bellwater Vault** opens: the Hollow cracked it while the city looked upriver.

## The vault

The **Bellwater Vault**, under the harbor bell.

- **What's sealed there:** **the Tolling Kraken**, a great harbor wild that once sank ships at every tide. The city's founders bound it and built the bell above it. Every toll is a lock turning.
- **What guards it:** the bell-founders' cipher (Isa reads it), drowned locks, and the kraken's brood.
- **The loot card (Isa's personal card):** the Tolling Kraken, a Water Manifest concept that punishes the enemy for casting.

## Boss

**The crowned harbor wild** that drowned Lowmere, chased back to Saltmere.

- **Rank:** Iron, against Wren's Copper. Afterwards, with the river contracts already in the Saltmere ledger, the hall sets the crew's **Iron assessment**: an Iron contract on the canals, witnessed by the Saltmere branch master, then a ceremony in the archive hall.
- **Crown:** **Tide**. At the end of each round it returns your most expensive creature to your hand. The story reason is the river pulling everything back to sea.
- **Side fight:** Pell and Brann hold its brood. Dove gets the people off the docks.
